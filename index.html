The Murali Story

Born in Andhra.
Made with tradition.

Andhra→Kitchen→Spices→Pickle→Your Table

Murali Pickles brings the rich character of Andhra prawn pickle to modern tables. Every batch starts with quality prawns, cleaned and prepared by hand.

Then simmered in a signature blend of chilli, salt and aromatics until the masala turns rich and glossy — the same way it's always been made.
